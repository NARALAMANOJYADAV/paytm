## Direction contract
THESIS: The event as one building of sliding planes: every page is a single room partitioned by flat rectangles and black frame lines, and moving through the site slides planes rather than stacking cards. Refuses the dark glass/gradient-card hero.
OWN-WORLD: White and cool-grey planes, 2px near-black frame lines, zero radius, no glow. Colour is rationed to planes that mean something: Paytm navy is the one large committed plane, Paytm sky marks the moving edge and the primary action, signal yellow marks the selected item, red only alerts. Archivo (wide display, normal body), tabular numerals.
STORY: Visitors see the title, date, venue and fee in one glance, see that Paytm and Microsoft speakers are teaching, and register. Participants, coordinators and admins work inside the same frame.
FIRST VIEWPORT: An asymmetric plane grid. A big navy plane on the left holds "Prompt to Production" in wide caps. White cells hold the date, time, venue, fee and seats. The poster plane is on the right. A sky Register plane sits at the lower left, with a yellow countdown cell. The frame lines run edge to edge.
